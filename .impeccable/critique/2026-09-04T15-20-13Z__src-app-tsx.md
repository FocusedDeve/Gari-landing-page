## Design Specificity Verdict

Copy is authored for this product; composition is not. Strip `content.ts` and a template remains that an oat-milk or granola brand could ship Monday.

- Navbar: generic. Hero: specific, done. MarqueeBand: half — "Sans gluten" argues to the wrong person (this audience has eaten gari for decades).
- Benefits: content specific, form entirely generic (four lucide icons in tinted `rounded-2xl` squares).
- Rituals: most specific after hero, because of its words (èba, adémè, huile rouge); form is a stock 3-up grid.
- Footer/CTA: generic and actively wrong. "Goûtez la différence" is the most replaceable sentence on the page, set at `clamp(2.5rem,9vw,7rem)` — biggest type, emptiest claim.

Reference system (donedrinks.com, 16967px walked): one geometry, no exceptions; colour rotates but roles never (orange = action only, never decoration); ZERO icons across 17000px, replaced by cut-out product and floating props; CTA every ~2500px and one per product card; proof honest about being thin (unattributed one-liners + FAQ, no seals).

Do NOT copy: the wave dividers (a liquid-pour metaphor belonging to a beverage; gari is dry, granular, "croustillant"); testimonials (PRODUCT.md forbids, none exist); founder portrait + timeline (no business); retail-availability bar (no retailer).

## Priority Issues

### 1. [P0] There is no product. The three formats are alt text.

`#produit` resolves to the hero. Sachet 500g / étui 500g / bocal 450g named only in `ProductTrio` alt attributes. Footer sells "le sachet de 1 kg" — a fourth format not in the range. `section#commander`, the primary conversion CTA, is nested inside `<footer>`, OUTSIDE `<main>`.

Breaks Nielsen 5 and 6 simultaneously; direct cause of the working-memory failure. PRODUCT.md principle 4 violated.

**Fix**: a `<Formats>` section between MarqueeBand and Benefits on the reference's "choose your flavor" pattern — three vertical `rounded-4xl` cards, each on its own tinted gradient from the palette, packshot on a contact shadow, name, weight, grind, the use it exists for, euro price, and its own Commander pill anchored bottom-right INSIDE the card.

**Command: shape** (then layout).

### 2. [P0] Commercial mechanics reject the stated audience at the decision moment.

`Footer.tsx`: "4 900 FCFA le sachet de 1 kg. Expédié sous 48 h depuis Lomé, partout au Togo." Plus FCFA in the hero button, `wa.me/22890000000`, Lomé market address as contact. No purchase question answered anywhere.

PRODUCT.md's user is in Europe/North America. Last thing the page says, so it owns peak-end memory. The page ends on a rejection.

**Fix**: euro prices per format; international shipping line; move the Lomé address out of Contact into an ORIGIN statement; drop +228 as ordering channel. Then a FAQ on `bg-cream` between Rituals and CTA, six questions (formats, quantity, countries, delay, shelf life after opening, damaged parcel). Where PRODUCT.md forbids a commitment, answer provisionally ("tarifs à confirmer") rather than inventing. Fix index.html's residual "Akpé Gari" title.

**Commands: clarify, then onboard.**

### 3. [P1] The mobile menu is a keyboard trap.

Mechanically confirmed: Escape does not close (`aria-expanded` stays "true" with focus on the close button); `querySelectorAll('[role]')` returns `[]` — no dialog semantics; background has neither `inert` nor `aria-hidden` (measured false on every escaped stop); focus escapes after the 5 menu links to the hero CTA then footer links at top=4993/5256, all invisible on a page that cannot scroll.

WCAG 2.4.3 / 2.4.11 failure; worst interaction defect on the site.

**Fix**: clickable scrim, Escape keydown handler, `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"`, focus trap with restore-on-close, full-height panel, 40x40 to 44x44.

**Command: harden.**

### 4. [P1] The accessibility floor fails systematically.

Measured over 124 nodes @1440 and 118 @390; colours resolved via canvas (Tailwind 4 emits oklch); engine validated against hand computation to ±0.03.

- **12 contrast failures.** Worst: "Prêt à croustiller" 2.47:1; two body blocks `bone/80` on clay 2.62:1; footer `<h2>`s and copyright 2.99:1; stat labels 4.03:1.
- **Single root cause**: `clay #C9743A` on `bone #FAF6EC` is exactly 3.22:1. Everything using the pair sits on the large-text floor with 0.22 margin — so at 390px, when `sm:text-2xl` drops to 20px, "Payé au juste prix" and the "D'OR" wordmark cross into failure.
- **10 of 15 touch targets under 44x44** at 390px, including the navbar Commander CTA (40x40) and six 20px-tall footer links.
- **4 of 6 `<section>` have no accessible name**; primary nav unlabelled; interactive borders (`border-ink/15`) at 1.38:1, below the 3:1 non-text minimum.
- No skip link: 6 tabs to reach hero content.

Assessment A caught three of these contrasts by eye; measurement found twelve, plus the entire targets/landmarks layer no visual review sees.

**Fix**: darken `clay` a step or stop using it for text on `bone`; raise `ink/45` and `ink/55`; 44px hit areas; name sections and nav; add a skip link.

**Command: audit.**

### 5. [P1] Benefits and Rituals are a generic DTC template wearing gari copy.

Four `lucide-react` icons in tinted squares; a ~350x750px dead quadrant bottom-right of Benefits where staggered columns stop above the sticky column; a "typographic accent card" (Gari Foto) containing NO typography, just a flame on yellow; no CTA in either section across 2600px of scroll.

The icon set is the tell that makes a reviewer say "template" — the reference has zero icons across 17000px and loses nothing. The void and the hollow card read as build errors, costing credibility on a page whose whole argument is craft.

**Fix**: replace icons with cut-out material props (a spoonful of granules, the cast-iron plate, a cassava root) floating in card margins; adopt the bento so colour-tile and photo-tile share one grid and one radius (also kills the void); make Gari Foto genuinely typographic as the README already promises.

**Command: bolder.**

## Cognitive Load: 5 of 8 fail

Chunking, grouping, visual hierarchy, working memory, progressive disclosure all fail. Biggest text is least informative; the price — the only decisive number — is 16px body copy. The MarqueeBand shows 14 pills simultaneously, in motion, in two directions, unranked: ten equal-weight claims is a word cloud, not a claim band.

**Riley (stress tester)**: Escape in the menu does nothing; tabs out of it into the hero CTA and the three footer socials, all invisible on an unscrollable page; clicks the CTA with no mail handler and nothing happens, no copyable fallback address; notes `<h2>` spent on "NAVIGATION" while the page's largest string is a `<p>`.

**Casey (one thumb)**: 6092px at 390px wide. Between hero CTA (y≈780) and footer CTA (y≈5400) the only order affordance is a 40x40 unlabelled bag icon, under the 44px minimum, visually indistinct from the hamburger beside it. Nine other targets fail with it.

**Afi, 38, Togolese, Villejuif** (PRODUCT.md persona): looks for shipping to France, euro price delivered, quantity, shelf life. Finds FCFA, "partout au Togo", a Lomé market address, a +228 WhatsApp. Concludes this is a Lomé shop that has not noticed her, closes the tab. Highest-value failure on the page. The claim band also tells her "Sans gluten" — after 38 years of gari, that sentence is addressed to someone else, and its presence signals the brand does not know who it is talking to.

## Minor Observations

- The cassava proof photo has an English "FRESH CASSAVA" sign staked in the basket, on a French page about Togo — reads as stock, damaging the exact credibility the section exists to build.
- "Coopérative de Kpalimé" appears once in a caption and nowhere else; body copy says "région des Plateaux". Unverifiable specificity introduced then dropped.
- All 6 images served over 2x displayed size at 1440; the three hero images 4.7-5.2x at 390px — 519 KiB above the fold, 64% of estimated ~791 KiB production first load. No `srcset`. Only one of three LCP visuals has `fetchpriority`.
- `ScrollProgress`, `SectionLabel`, the wordmark accent and the CTA block all share `clay`, so the colour carries no meaning. The reference's discipline (one colour, actions only, ever) is free to adopt and would make every CTA land harder without enlarging a button.
- `ShoppingBag` icon on a `mailto:` link promises a cart that does not exist.
- Social links point at `https://instagram.com` / `https://facebook.com` — logged-out home pages.
- Zero console errors, zero failed requests, zero horizontal overflow across 8 widths. Technical base is sound.
- Reduced motion verified excellent: under `prefers-reduced-motion` exactly ONE element still moves (the scroll-bound progress bar); a sweep for any element at `opacity < 0.5` carrying text returned zero.

## Questions to Consider

1. What if the page's spine were the three formats rather than the four benefits? sachet to èba, étui to boisson glacée, bocal to croustillant.
2. The reference sells to people who do not know its product; you sell to people who know yours better than you do. Why is your page structured like a page that explains?
3. What is the honest substitute for testimonials? Your strongest available proof is something the reference lacks: a showable process.
4. The reference's transitions are liquid because it sells liquid. What is gari's material transition — scatter, sift, grain, the crackle of a hot plate?
5. If ordering must stay a `mailto:` for now, why is the email empty? A prefilled body with format, quantity and destination country would fix three heuristic failures for ~15 lines in `Footer.tsx`.
